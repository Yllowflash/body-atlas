Notes:
- The 40 organ models, full skeleton, musculature and skin meshes in
  models/*.glb are merged (not recentered, rotated or rescaled) from the
  BodyParts3D 4.0 Wavefront OBJ releases:
    isa_BP3D_4.0_obj_99.zip and partof_BP3D_4.0_obj_99.zip
  via the mapping files isa_element_parts.txt / partof_element_parts.txt.
- Coordinates are preserved exactly as published (millimetres, Z-up,
  +X = subject's left). The app converts Z-up to Y-up with a single root
  transform and normalizes the whole scene as one group.
- (Stale header comments inside the original .obj files mention an older
  CC Attribution-ShareAlike 2.1 Japan license text; the current license
  page for the database, last updated 2025/02/27, specifies
  CC Attribution 4.0 International, which is what this project follows.)
